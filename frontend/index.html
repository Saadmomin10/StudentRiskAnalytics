📌 Risk Concentration

370 of 500 students are classified as High or Medium risk, representing 74% of the student population.

🎓 Previous GPA

Previous GPA shows a strong difference across risk groups, with Low-risk students averaging substantially higher GPA than High-risk students.

📚 Study Behavior

Average study hours increase from High-risk students to Medium-risk and then Low-risk students.

📅 Attendance

Attendance averages are relatively close across risk groups, showing that attendance alone is not enough to explain student risk.

📝 Assignments

Assignment completion provides useful information, but the risk classification is multidimensional.

🚨 Intervention

Students should be prioritized using a combination of academic performance, study behavior and engagement indicators.
